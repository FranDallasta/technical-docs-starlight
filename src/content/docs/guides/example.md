**Ghost** is an open-source, professional publishing platform built on a modern Node.js technology stack, designed for teams who need power, flexibility, and performance. This is a excellent alternative for content creators who want to focus mainly on publishing their work, monetize their audience or are looking for an alternative to WordPress.

## Who is Ghost for?

- Independent bloggers and writers  
- Digital magazines and media publishers  
- Membership sites and newsletters  
- Businesses looking for a modern corporate blog  

## Key Features

- **Built-in memberships and subscriptions** to help you monetize your audience.  
- **Open-source and customizable**, with a RESTful JSON API and webhook support.  
- **Markdown-based editor with rich content blocks ("cards")** for images, videos, embeds, and custom HTML.  
- **Advanced SEO** features built-in by default, with no need for third-party plugins.  
- **Integrations** with Stripe, Zapier, Slack, and many more.  
- **Theme Marketplace**, with the ability to build your own custom themes using Handlebars or an external front-end.  
- **High performance** thanks to a modern Node.js architecture, with best practices for security.  

## Advantages

- Full control over content and website appearance.  
- Active open-source community and optional professional support.  
- Scales well from small personal blogs to large editorial platforms.  

## Disadvantages

- Requires some technical knowledge for installation and maintenance (self-hosted server).  
- Some advanced features are only available through a Ghost(Pro) subscription.
